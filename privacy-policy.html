About This Service

Social Media Auto Publisher is a personal automation tool designed to publish images to connected social media accounts.

Information We Process

The service may process images selected for publication, image URLs, file names, publishing statuses, and authentication information required to connect social media accounts.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to manage and publish images to social media platforms selected by the account owner.

Third-Party Services

Images and publishing requests may be sent to connected platforms, including TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, and X, according to the account owner's instructions. Those platforms handle information under their own privacy policies.

Data Security and Retention

Access credentials should be stored securely and are used only to operate the automation. Information is retained only as needed to operate and maintain the service.
